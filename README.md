# Scam Watch Daily — Archive

Every edition of Zain Aziz's daily Scam Watch Daily briefing: top scam stories
involving the major platforms, new and emerging scam tactics, research on
fraud and social engineering, victim voices, and a daily safety tip.

- Site: https://zanyaziz.github.io/scam-watch-daily/
- Newest edition first, 10 per page. Each edition page links back to the archive.

## How editions get here

The daily run saves the full edition markdown, converts it with
`scripts/convert_editions.py` into `editions/YYYY-MM-DD.html`, rebuilds the
landing page with `scripts/build_index.py`, and pushes. The October 28, 2026
edition is the exported artifact page (no markdown file was saved that day).

## Archive gaps

No edition was produced on October 1, 2026. The September 27, October 6,
October 8, and October 9, 2026 editions were delivered in chat but their full
text was not saved for the archive.
